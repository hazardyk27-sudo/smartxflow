# Draw Trap V1 — Shadow Tracking

Frozen on: 2026-10-15
Status: SHADOW / RESEARCH_CANDIDATE
Production status: NOT PROMOTED

## Frozen definition

A fixture qualifies when all conditions are true:

1. Evaluate the same HOME/AWAY side at T-6 and T-3.
2. That side's odds fall by at least 5% from T-6 to T-3.
3. That side's T-3 money share is 40%–65% inclusive.
4. That side's T-3 odds are 2.00–2.40 inclusive.
5. The opponent's T-3 odds are >=2.75.
6. Shadow selection is DRAW at the real observed T-3 draw odds.

## Discovery sample

Research period: 2026-10-09 through 2026-10-14.
Exploratory qualifying cases: 9.
Exploratory draws: 8/9.

Important: the 2.00–2.40 selected-side odds band was discovered post-hoc on the same historical sample. Therefore 8/9 is discovery evidence only and is not independent validation.

## Prospective tracking protocol

- Do not tune or widen/narrow the frozen thresholds while shadow data is being collected.
- Record every future qualifying case, not only winners.
- Preserve T-6 odds, T-3 selected-side odds, T-3 selected-side money share, opponent T-3 odds, T-3 draw odds, snapshot ages and final result.
- Evaluate flat 1u draw P/L and ROI using the real observed T-3 draw odds.
- Review at 10 new prospective cases, then 20, then 30.
- Any changed threshold becomes a new version and cannot rewrite V1 history.
